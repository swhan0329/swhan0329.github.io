Pangyo Dictionary

A light Korean glossary of 240 IT, software development, AI, and startup terms for people who want to vibe code with AI and talk more comfortably with developers, planners, and startup teams.

It is a glossary of "Pangyo-speak", but it also works as a friendly field guide to developer communication for non-developers building with AI.

Who It Helps

People vibe coding with AI

Even when AI writes most of the code, you still meet words like spec, API, deploy, QA, and bug. This glossary helps you ask clearer questions and review work with more confidence.

People working with developers and planners

Learn common meeting and Slack expressions such as align, sync, confirm, due date, blocker, and follow-up through lightweight Korean examples.

People new to startup language

Skim practical product, data, and funding terms such as MVP, PoC, PMF, valuation, runway, funnel, and retention.
